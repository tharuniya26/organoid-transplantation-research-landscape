# Evidence quality framework

The repository avoids a fabricated universal score across biologically incompatible designs. Instead, `results/evidence_quality_summary.csv` reports transparent domains: model reported, identifiable comparator/control design, engraftment assessment, functional outcome reporting, safety assessment, clinical applicability, and an appraisal note.

This is a signalling framework, not a formal risk-of-bias judgment. For a systematic review, design-specific tools should be applied: SYRCLE-aligned domains for animal experiments, ARRIVE reporting checks, and an appropriate clinical tool if human studies become eligible. Sample size, randomization, blinding, attrition, preregistration, follow-up, immune response, and replication require full-text extraction and are not inferred from abstracts.

All current reports provide indirect preclinical evidence. A positive domain says the feature was reported or assessed; it does not certify low bias, reproducibility, or clinical validity.
