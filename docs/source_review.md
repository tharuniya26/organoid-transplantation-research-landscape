## Source A: published review

The article surveys organoid derivation, RPE-cell transplantation, intestinal and colonic organoids, brain organoids, and translational barriers. It is useful as prior research context and a source of candidate references, but it is not treated as a primary transplantation study in the evidence database. Several passages blur organoid transplantation with transplantation of differentiated cells or cell sheets. The new analysis therefore re-screened primary records against an explicit definition.

The RPE clinical studies discussed in the article involve differentiated RPE cells or monolayer patches, not complete retinal organoids. They are scientifically relevant but ineligible for the organoid-graft database. The reported Japanese intestinal/colonic clinical activity was not converted into an outcome row because a press report or trial description is not a peer-reviewed primary outcome publication.

## Source B: kidney-organoid poster

The poster covers kidney organoid differentiation, nephron-like and glomerular structures, vascularization following mouse implantation, limited collecting-duct integration, off-target cells, immaturity, and reproducibility. These concepts informed the kidney background and barrier coding.

The left-hand case presentation is a conventional deceased-donor kidney transplant after long-term dialysis (Okamoto et al., 2009). It is not a human kidney-organoid transplant and is excluded from the evidence database. The poster’s assignment branding, cohort details, BMS and Northumbria logos, and third-party figures are not reused in public outputs.

## Main scientific corrections carried forward

- Organoid grafts, differentiated-cell transplants, organ sheets, fetal tissue, and whole-organ transplantation are distinct interventions.
- All 15 studies in the curated dataset are preclinical in vivo experiments; none demonstrates an approved human organoid therapy.
- Vascular perfusion or tracer handling in an ectopic animal graft does not equal organ-level replacement function.
- Missing outcome reporting is not coded as a negative result.
- Brain-organoid transplantation studies are primarily model systems and raise special animal-chimera ethical questions.
