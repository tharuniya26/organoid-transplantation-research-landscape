# Study selection

The supplied review and poster yielded candidate concepts and citations. PubMed title/abstract searches and backward checks identified primary transplantation experiments. After removal of reviews, in vitro-only models, conventional organ transplants, isolated-cell therapies, and non-primary announcements, 15 primary reports were retained.

The most consequential exclusions were: the Okamoto et al. conventional kidney-transplant case; clinical RPE cell/monolayer studies; review articles by Freedman and Dekel and by Kalejaiye and colleagues; and the published review itself. These records inform context but are not counted as organoid-graft experiments.

Because screening was performed as a single-reviewer structured map and not from a complete database export, no PRISMA flow count is claimed. A future systematic-review upgrade should archive all retrieved records, use duplicate independent screening, and publish reason-level exclusions.
