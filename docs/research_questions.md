# Research questions and objectives

**Main question:** How does published evidence for organoid transplantation differ across organoid types, stem-cell sources, experimental models, engraftment outcomes, and translational barriers?

The evidence map identifies eligible primary studies; curates traceable records; classifies organoid type, cell source, model, and evidence stage; distinguishes engraftment, vascularization, function, and safety; constructs a source-backed heterogeneous network; maps translational evidence; and identifies gaps. It does not estimate clinical success probability or claim systematic-review completeness.
