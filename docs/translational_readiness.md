# Translational evidence interpretation

The matrix uses `Supported`, `Unsupported`, `Unassessed`, and `Uncertain`. It is intentionally descriptive and non-numeric. “Supported” means at least one included study documents the evidence category; it does not mean the category is complete, independently replicated, clinically effective, or regulator-approved.

The corpus supports preclinical engraftment for most mapped categories and vascular integration for kidney, intestinal, liver, and brain grafts. Retinal organoid sheets show functional visual endpoints but vascularization was not a central coded endpoint. Safety assessment is sparse. Human clinical investigation is unassessed across every included organoid category. Manufacturing/scale-up evidence is uncertain where off-target tissue, quality control, or variability is explicitly documented.

No category is ranked. The most important cross-cutting gaps are prospective safety, immune compatibility, standardized manufacturing, organ-scale function, physiological drainage or output pathways, durable host integration, and human trial evidence.
