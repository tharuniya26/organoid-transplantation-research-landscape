# Source claim verification

| Claim encountered in supplied material | Assessment | Project treatment |
|---|---|---|
| The Okamoto 2009 case demonstrates human kidney-organoid transplantation | **Incorrect conflation** | Excluded; it is conventional donor-kidney transplantation |
| Human RPE trials are complete retinal-organoid transplants | **Incorrect equivalence** | Excluded from organoid-graft database; discussed as related cell/patch therapy |
| Kidney organoid grafts can vascularize and show glomerular tracer handling in mice | **Supported preclinically** | Coded with van den Berg 2018/2020 and related studies |
| Kidney organoids currently replace mature kidney function | **Not supported** | Explicitly rejected; no collecting/urinary outlet and limited scale/function remain |
| Human brain/cortical organoids can survive and integrate in rodent brain | **Supported preclinically** | Coded with Mansour 2018 and Revah 2022; no clinical-therapy claim |
| A human intestinal/colonic organoid intervention has established efficacy | **Not verified** | No efficacy claim; unpublished/early feasibility descriptions excluded from outcomes |
| The published review is peer reviewed | **Not verified** | Journal publication is verified; peer-review process is not claimed |
| The review has a DOI | **Not verified** | No DOI recorded |

Verification relied on the supplied PDFs, PubMed records linked in the dataset, and the journal’s official issue page. “Not verified” is deliberately distinct from “false.”
