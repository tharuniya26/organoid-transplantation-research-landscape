# Eligibility criteria

## Include

- Primary experimental research.
- A three-dimensional self-organizing organoid, organ-bud, or organoid-derived tissue sheet is transplanted into a living recipient.
- In vivo engraftment, survival, maturation, vascularization, function, safety, or host integration is evaluated.
- Sufficient bibliographic identity is available (title plus DOI or PMID).

## Exclude

- Reviews, perspectives, protocols without transplantation results, and news reports.
- Conventional whole-organ transplantation.
- Isolated-cell transplantation, including RPE-cell suspensions, when no organoid-derived 3D tissue is grafted.
- Spheroids lacking documented organoid self-organization, organ-on-chip systems without implantation, and purely in vitro work.
- Fetal-tissue grafts not generated as organoids.
- Human feasibility announcements without a peer-reviewed primary outcome report.

## Evidence stage vocabulary

`In vitro`, `Ex vivo`, `Preclinical in vivo`, `Human feasibility`, `Interventional clinical trial`, and `Approved therapy`. All included records currently map to `Preclinical in vivo`.
